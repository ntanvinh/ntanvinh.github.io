About LSSoft

One small studio, building things meant to last.

LSSoft designs and builds focused apps for everyday tasks — audio, video, personal finance, small utilities. No bloat, no dark patterns, just software that respects your time and your data.

The name

LS stands for "Little Sand" — one small grain among countless others.

A grain of sand doesn't need to stand out. It just settles where it belongs, and quietly adds a little weight to the ground.

That's how we try to build software: not the loudest, not the biggest — just useful, one small app at a time. Enough small grains, placed with care, become solid ground. LSSoft still carries that spirit, from the very first grain.

Our philosophy

Six principles behind every release.

Simple by design

Keep only what truly matters so the experience stays clear, calm, and easy to use.

Fast in every interaction

Each interaction should respond quickly and never break the user's flow.

Privacy first

Ask for the minimum, request access at the right time, and keep users in control.

Built to last

Do fewer things, but build them carefully so they stay reliable over time.

Consistent and calm

Interfaces and behaviors should feel familiar so people do not have to relearn the app.

Always improving

Listen to real feedback, fix the small frictions, and keep moving forward step by step.

Founder

Built by one person, for real use

LSSoft is built and maintained by a single independent developer — no big team, no growth-at-all-costs roadmap. Just steady, careful work: shipping what's needed, fixing what's broken, and building for people who use these apps every day.

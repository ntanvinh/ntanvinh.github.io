Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

LSSoft ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications (including but not limited to VMoney Mate, VZuiZe, and other LSSoft products) collect, use, and protect your data.

Our Privacy-First Philosophy

We follow a privacy-first philosophy:

- No data selling

- Minimal data collection

- Transparent usage

2. Data We Collect

Depending on features you use, we may collect:

 

a. Account Information

- Email address (via Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In)

- User ID

 

b. Usage Data & Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics. Collected data may include:

- App usage (screens, actions)

- Device info (model, OS version)

- Crash reports

 

c. Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob. This may collect:

- Device identifiers

- Approximate location

- Ad interaction data

 

d. User Content

- Notes, bookmarks, audio files (stored locally or synced)

- Preferences (theme, language)

 

e. Cloud Sync Data

We use Google Firebase Firestore / Storage to store:

- Playback progress

- User content (if sync enabled)

3. How We Use Data

We use data to:

- Provide and improve app functionality

- Sync data across devices

- Analyze usage to improve experience

- Serve relevant ads (if enabled)

 

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data Sharing

We may share data with:

- Google Firebase (analytics, cloud)

- Google AdMob (ads)

 

We do not share data for resale.

5. Data Storage & Security

Data may be stored:

- Locally on your device

- On cloud services (Firebase)

 

We use industry-standard security practices.

6. Permissions

The app may request:

- Storage access (import files)

- Network access (sync & online content)

- Advertising ID (for ads)

 

Permissions are used only for intended functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

This app uses:

- Google Firebase (Analytics, Firestore, Storage)

- Google AdMob (Advertising)

- Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In (Authentication)

 

Each service has its own privacy policy.

8. User Rights

You can:

- Delete your data (local or account)

- Disable analytics (where applicable)

- Opt out of personalized ads

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy periodically.

EPUB and PDF in one library

Small apps. A world of possibilities.

VBook Teller is a local-first ebook reader for EPUB and PDF, combining reading, text-to-speech narration, folder organization, and reading statistics in one clean library. Import books directly on your device or transfer them from your computer over local Wi-Fi, no cloud required. Built for readers who want a fast, private, and flexible way to read and listen to their personal collection.

INSIDE THE APP

Thought through. Every detail.

Every feature. One seamless experience.

01 /

EPUB and PDF in one library

Keep novels, PDFs, and study materials in a single organized library, no need for separate apps depending on file format.

02 /

Import books over local Wi-Fi, no cloud

Start a temporary local server and upload EPUB or PDF files straight from your computer to your phone, all within your own network.

03 /

PDF reading built for phones

Keep the original page layout for scans and manga, or switch to Reflow mode to read text-heavy PDFs more comfortably on a small screen.

04 /

Listen with narration that follows along

Built-in text-to-speech reads your book aloud and highlights each sentence as it goes, so you can switch between reading and listening anytime.

05 /

A reading experience you can make your own

Adjust theme and layout, jump between chapters, and save bookmarks without breaking your reading flow.

06 /

Track your reading time and streaks

See books opened and completed, total reading time, daily average, and your current streak at a glance.

07 /

See your reading habits in detail

A weekly activity calendar plus a breakdown of your library by format and progress shows exactly how your reading habit is building up.

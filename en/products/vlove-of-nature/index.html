INSIDE THE APP

Thought through. Every detail.

Every feature. One seamless experience.

01 /

Pause With Nature Scenes

Take a quiet pause with wide, silent nature video. Desktop viewing gives each landscape room to fill your screen; move among scenes from forests and shores to mountains and islands.

02 /

Explore Plants for Home and Office

Browse plant profiles for homes, desks, and offices. Each profile brings together the plant name, growing conditions, care notes, and cautions to help you decide what to learn more about.

03 /

Get Plant Ideas for Your Space

Choose a desk, office, room, balcony, or garden; adjust the light and available width to see plant suggestions with practical care notes and cautions.

04 /

Compare Plant Profiles Side by Side

Compare up to three profiles across space, light, mature size, care effort, and cautions. Unknown details remain visible so you can judge what needs checking.

05 /

Read Source-Linked Nature Facts

Read a short fact connected to the current landscape and open its cited source to learn more. The collection still needs expert and translation review.

06 /

Find Plants by Name and Conditions

Search by common or scientific name, then filter by space and light to narrow the list. See matching profiles at a glance; local suitability still needs to be checked.

07 /

Follow Plant Care Guides

Open supported plant profiles for step-by-step notes on potting, light, watering, feeding, and common issues. Review the sources and safety notes alongside the guide.

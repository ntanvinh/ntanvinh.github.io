INSIDE THE APP

Thought through. Every detail.

Every feature. One seamless experience.

01 /

Watch Window Activity

Monitor a whole window or selected regions. See when screen content is changing or has gone quiet, so you can tell whether an AI task may have finished.

02 /

See AI Account Quotas

Check remaining usage across Claude Code, ChatGPT/Codex, and Gemini accounts in one compact view.

03 /

Feed Prompts When AI Windows Are Idle

Queue prompts for chosen chat inputs and let Auto Feed send them when the target AI window is inactive.

04 /

Organize Todo Prompts

Group tasks into categories, choose what is next, and push a Todo into an AI input when you are ready.

05 /

Clean Storage for Developers

Review developer caches, simulator data, Docker resources, and generated project files. See why an item is marked Safe or Review before choosing what to remove.

06 /

Capture and Annotate Screenshots

Capture a screen, window, or region, then add arrows, text, highlights, or pixelation.

07 /

Monitor App Resources

Compare overall CPU and memory use with per-app RAM and CPU. Set thresholds to make high-use apps easier to spot.

08 /

Pick Colors and Keep Palettes

Pick a color from the screen, keep named palettes, and copy colors in formats that fit your work.

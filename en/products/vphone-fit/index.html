Smart rep tracking

Small apps. A world of possibilities.

VPhone Fit turns your phone into a sensor-powered rep counter for practical home workouts, while also combining flashcard learning and guided Zen breathing. It is made for busy people who want one simple daily routine for movement, focus, and calm.

INSIDE THE APP

Thought through. Every detail.

Every feature. One seamless experience.

01 /

Smart rep tracking

Use the phone you already have to track movement-based exercises, earn XP, and build workout streaks without a smartwatch.

02 /

Train while you learn

Choose an exercise and a flashcard deck, then complete reps and review cards together in one focused training session.

03 /

Hands-free voice review

Reveal answers automatically and use spoken SRS choices to review flashcards without touching the screen.

04 /

Audio-rich flashcards

Study with images, pronunciation, multiple audio tracks, and clear Again, Hard, Good, and Easy review controls.

05 /

Import and manage decks

Import flashcard collections, organize saved decks, and keep your learning library ready for the next session.

06 /

See learning progress

Review session targets, learned cards, new cards, due cards, completion, and deck statistics at a glance.

07 /

Build your breathing rhythm

Choose a breathing pattern and adjust inhale, hold, exhale, and session duration for a calmer reset.

08 /

Guided Zen sessions

Follow clear on-screen inhale, hold, and exhale cues with a visual breathing guide that keeps the session easy to follow.

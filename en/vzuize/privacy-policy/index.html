Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01/01/2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application VZuiZe ("App"), developed and operated by Nguyen Tan Vinh ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

a. Personal Information: We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) from any users, especially children.

b. Automatically Collected Information: When you use the App, we may collect non-personal data including:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

c. Advertising Data: The App may use third-party advertising services (e.g., Google AdMob), which may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • Interaction data with ads

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Improve App performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical crashes
  • Display appropriate advertisements
  • Analyze anonymous usage trends

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party SDKs for analytics and advertising. We ensure these services are configured to comply with child protection regulations:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Configured to serve non-personalized and family-safe ads for child audiences.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Analytics): Used strictly for performance monitoring and anonymous usage metrics.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties.
  • We may share anonymous data with our verified service providers (analytics, ads) solely for the operation of the App.
  • We may disclose information if strictly required by law or legal process.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable and industry-standard measures to protect user data. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children’s Privacy & Google Play Families Policy Compliance

Because our App's target age group includes children, we are fully committed to protecting children's privacy and strictly adhere to the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not knowingly collect, transmit, or store any personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • Family-Safe Advertisements: All third-party ad networks (Google AdMob) used in this App are restricted to using the "Tag for Child-Directed Treatment" (TFCD) API. This ensures that personal identifiers are not used for ad targeting, behavioral tracking, or profiling when the user is a child.
  • Data Safety Commitment: We explicitly commit to safeguarding children's data, ensuring that all data practices within the App meet the safety requirements set by global privacy laws and Google Play's strict family standards.

7. User Rights

Depending on your location, you and your parents/legal guardians have the right to:

  • Access the data collected by the App
  • Request the permanent deletion of any data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Please contact us via the email below to execute any of these rights.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with new regulations. Any changes will be updated directly on this page with a revised effective date.
